Publication and recommendation boundary

  • Routes are shown only when they exist in the historical route directory and their explicit effective publication state is allowed.
  • Audited literature routes use the deterministic override registry rather than a generic historical “published” label.
  • Routes marked ineligible for reading are excluded from discovery.
  • Draft, private, unpublished and archived states remain excluded by the discovery configuration.
  • The route order uses local deterministic filters; it is not personalised AI.
  • No remote analytics, recommendation service or visitor profile is used.
