Phase 20 expands useful public coverage while preserving strict devotional
publication boundaries.

- Expands discoverable temples from 16 to 24:

  - 18 official-identity regional records.
- Adds eight official HR&CE identities: Thiruporur, Vadapalani,
  Siruvapuri/Chinnambedu, Kandhakottam, Thiruverumbur, Pollachi, Kalipatti
  and Sivanmalai.
- Keeps history, sthala puranam, rituals, schedules, contacts, travel advice
  and documentary imagery unpublished until reviewed.
- Publishes honest 1,300-song programme accounting:
  - 12 source-linked songs published;
  - 39 unchecked OCR drafts quarantined;
  - 18 empty shells excluded from song counts.
- Guarantees the core song and temple cards remain visible even when an older
  animation stylesheet is cached.
- Corrects the listening queue to 4 mantra/Kavasam records plus 12
  Thiruppugazh records.
- Adds clear startup, unsupported-browser and speech-error status messages.
- Rotates offline caches and includes the new governed contracts in the
  deterministic GitHub Pages artifact.

## Boundaries

- No sacred text was generated.
- No OCR-only text was promoted.
- No third-party devotional recording was copied.
- Device speech is labelled as device speech, not human devotional audio.
- The 1,300 figure is a programme target, not a published-song count.
