Language Access

மொழி அணுகல்

Tamil and English are the governed bilingual foundation. Devotional lyrics, meanings and historical statements will not be silently machine-translated and presented as verified scholarship.

Return homePublication policy

Available now

  • Tamil + English: governed bilingual presentation.
  • Tamil focus: visually prioritises marked Tamil text without deleting English context.
  • English focus: visually reduces marked Tamil text while preserving the original.
  • Daily practice: the Daily Murugan Darshan keeps Tamil and English together while source-controlled mantras and songs remain unchanged.

Verified language-pack roadmap

International access will be added in reviewed stages.

Indian languages

Hindi, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Bengali and Sinhala packs require human-reviewed devotional terminology.

International languages

French, German, Spanish, Japanese and Chinese packs require page-level translation review and source attribution.

What will never be automatic

Canonical Tamil lyrics, quotations, names, temple evidence and rights statements remain source-controlled.

Browser translation

Your browser may offer automatic translation for general interface text. Treat those results as convenience translations, not canonical devotional or scholarly editions.
