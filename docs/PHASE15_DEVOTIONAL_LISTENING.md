# Phase 15 — Devotional Listening and Missing-Song Governance

## Outcome

Phase 15 turns the existing verified song and device read-aloud datasets into a
purposeful devotional experience while making popular missing-song work visible.

## Devotee experience

- Six Tamil-first listening and reflection paths.
- Verified reading-route links and device text-to-speech only.
- Browser-local practice completion, private note and prayer queue.
- Motion that respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Keyboard, screen-reader status and forced-colour support.

## Missing-song experience

- Fourteen requested song identities are searchable.
- Visitors can mark local priorities and copy an evidence template.
- AI-assisted local search returns missing titles as source requests.
- Missing records never contain lyrics, remembered fragments or recording URLs.

## Non-negotiable boundary

Public familiarity is not evidence of redistribution rights. No commercial recording,
modern copyrighted lyrics or unverified OCR text is promoted by this release. New
full-song publication still requires an exact edition, human Tamil review and a
recorded rights decision.

## Production contract

The deterministic Pages builder and validator require both new routes, data files,
schemas, scripts and styles. The service-worker revision is rotated so existing PWA
visitors cannot remain on the Phase 14 cache.
